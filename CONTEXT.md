# Ring World War Domain Glossary

## Last Rotation

The provisional name for the final era in which the ring can still perform one habitat-scale corrective operation. Whether the name is used by people inside the setting or only as the narrative title remains undecided.

## Meridian Compact

A coalition of permanent arc-cities, civil governments, industrial families, and military commands committed to preserving the ring's present population and inhabited districts. Its unity is practical rather than ideological, and its internal factions disagree over who can be protected.

## Axiom Choir

A mobile society descended from maintenance guilds, sensor operators, and people integrated to varying degrees with distributed machine systems. It is committed to preserving a viable future beyond the ring, but has no settled moral consensus about selection, sacrifice, or what form that future should take.

## Anchor Protocol

The Meridian Compact's proposed use of the ring's remaining corrective capacity to stabilize angular velocity, synchronize essential habitat systems, and preserve selected inhabited arcs. It does not stop the ring's rotation. Its achievable duration and ultimate viability are provisional.

## Migration Protocol

The Axiom Choir's proposed use of selected ring sectors, stored momentum, and the ring's remaining corrective capacity to launch a smaller population, biological archive, and machine civilization toward another star. The destination, carrying capacity, and selection process remain provisional.

## Solar Filament

The ring's central light and energy source. Its growing instability drives the Last Rotation crisis. Its exact origin, physical construction, and failure mode are deliberately unresolved.

## Shadow-Square Network

The moving panel network that regulates light and climate across the ring's inhabited surface. Its synchronization is failing. The network's complete extent and original control hierarchy remain provisional.

## Spinal Network / Spinal Node / Spinal Alignment

The Spinal Network is the surviving ring-wide control and structural-correction system capable of authorizing one final habitat-scale operation. A Spinal Node is a territorial access point through which a theater authority can command part of that network. Surviving routing records identify some Nodes as operational pairs; controlling both members establishes a reversible local Spinal Alignment. The Network's total node count, complete hierarchy, relationship among pairs, and final-protocol authorization requirements remain unresolved.

## Bastion

A fortified theater headquarters that anchors command, fabrication, and territorial control. Losing a Bastion decides a local theater, not necessarily the entire war.

## Scrith Salvage

Recoverable structural material taken from ruins, damaged infrastructure, and designated ring sectors. It is both the principal material of war and part of the habitat the factions claim to be saving. The term's origin and the material's exact composition remain provisional.

## Chord Shot

A strategic missile trajectory that crosses the ring's open interior rather than following the inhabited surface. It is fast, conspicuous, destructive, and dependent on specialized launch infrastructure. Extensions of the principle to transportation remain provisional.
