# Third-party asset credits

## Ondrasaur low-poly building collection

The static visual-dressing models in `public/assets/buildings/ondrasaur/` are
adapted from **“Star Wars inspired low poly buildings”** by **ondrasaur**.

- Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/star-wars-inspired-low-poly-buildings-db883b9fd31f438e94abd3cb8cb3ead5
- Licence: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- Changes: exported as glTF binary (`.glb`), split into independently placeable
  groups, and renamed for RingWorldWar’s static visual-dressing catalogue.

The assets are decorative only. They do not participate in collision,
navigation, line of sight, targeting, resources, saves, or simulation state.
